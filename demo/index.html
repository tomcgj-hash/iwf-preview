    Three things a buyer should test

    1. Does the route survive a floor change

    Pick the cinema on Level 2 from the Level 1 entrance. A real engine sends the visitor to a vertical core, switches the map, and continues on the destination floor. A drawing of a line does not.

    2. Is there a step-free option

    Tick the box and the escalator edges disappear from the graph, so the visitor is routed through lifts only. Accessibility is a property of the walk graph, not a label added at the end.

    3. Does the turn list make sense on arrival

    A turn list that says "continue 40 m" fails in a corridor with four identical shopfronts. Our production pages attach landmark references to each step, which is why the map and the tenant content have to live in one system.
