Commitments

Six clauses that decide whether a project works

Each of these appears in the contract, not only on this page. If one of them is missing from a competing proposal, ask why.

01

Acceptance before sign-off

Accuracy measured per zone on the finished system against a protocol agreed up front. A zone that misses its target is a defect, not a discussion.

02

Support targets in writing

24-hour reply on any enquiry, 4-hour response on a class A incident (wrong route, kiosk down, data exposure), with named escalation contacts.

03

Map updates during the term

Tenant, department and event changes handled within the agreed cycle, with a named content owner on both sides.

04

Data ownership and exit

Map data, tenant content and analytics export in an open format on exit, then our copies are deleted with written confirmation.

05

Hardware warranty

12 months on supplied kiosks, gateways and beacons; software updates included during the support term.

06

No claims without permission

No client name, logo or performance figure appears on this site or in a proposal without written consent.

Who does the work

Five roles, not one account manager

The same team that scopes the project runs the survey, the calibration and the acceptance test. Your proposal names the delivery lead and the support contact with direct details.

Map production
Surveys the plans, draws floors, rooms and points of interest, builds the walkable graph and runs the map quality check before deployment.
Positioning engineering
Chooses the technology mix, lays out gateways, tests coverage on site and calibrates the accuracy that the acceptance test will measure.
Integration engineering
Writes the interface work against your app, parking system, CRM, signage CMS or directory feed, and documents every endpoint.
Delivery lead
Owns the schedule, the acceptance test and the handover. Named in your proposal with a direct contact.
Support desk
Handles incidents and content changes after go-live, with the severity definitions above.

Data and exit

Leaving is a documented process

  • Map data, tenant content and analytics export in an open format within 30 days of termination.
  • Processor copies deleted within 90 days of handover, confirmed in writing.
  • On-premise delivery available where visitor data must not leave the venue network.
  • Data processing agreement and model privacy notice are deliverables, not extras.

Boundaries

What we will not claim

  • We do not claim certifications, awards or client logos that are not in our own name.
  • We do not promise accuracy figures before the survey and the calibration walk.
  • We do not sell asset tracking, RTLS hardware or camera-based identity tracking.
  • We do not present a planning configuration as a delivered project.
